Mercedes-Benz Stadium

Atlanta will host World Cup matches at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, one of the most modern multi-purpose stadiums in the United States.

The venue is known for its large capacity, advanced roof design, and strong atmosphere for major football events. Its central location and excellent transport links make Atlanta an important stop for fans travelling across the tournament.
