Introduction

The FIFA World Cup is the biggest football tournament in the world. It brings together the best national teams from across the globe to compete for the prestigious World Cup trophy. The tournament is organized by FIFA and is held once every four years.

Millions of fans from different countries watch the matches and support their favorite teams. The event celebrates not only football but also different cultures and traditions from around the world. Teams must first qualify through regional competitions before reaching the final tournament.

Winning the World Cup is considered the greatest achievement in international football. Legendary players and historic moments have made the tournament one of the most exciting sporting events on the planet.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup will be hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico and will include 48 teams.
