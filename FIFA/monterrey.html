Estadio BBVA

Monterrey will host matches at Estadio BBVA, a modern stadium with a strong reputation for atmosphere and design.

The venue reflects the city's deep sports culture and its importance in Mexican football. It will be one of the key locations for fans visiting northern Mexico during the World Cup.
